# Helios H10 Home Battery: Owner's Guide

## Overview
The Helios H10 stores surplus energy from rooftop solar panels for use in the evening or during a grid outage. Each unit has a usable capacity of 10.2 kilowatt-hours and a continuous output of 5 kilowatts, with a peak output of 7.5 kilowatts for ten seconds to start motors such as pumps and compressors. Up to four units can be stacked on one inverter.

## Installation
The battery must be installed by a certified electrician. It can be mounted indoors or outdoors, but it must be kept out of direct sunlight and at least 90 centimetres away from gas meters. The operating temperature range is minus 10 to 45 degrees Celsius. The unit weighs 114 kilograms, so a concrete floor or a reinforced wall is required.

## Operating modes
In self-consumption mode the battery charges from solar surplus and discharges whenever the house draws power from the grid. Time-of-use mode charges from the grid during cheap off-peak hours and discharges during peak tariffs. Backup reserve mode keeps a configurable share of capacity, 20 percent by default, untouched so that it is available during outages.

## Outages
When the grid fails, the battery switches to backup power in under 20 milliseconds, so most electronics keep running without interruption. During an outage the solar panels keep charging the battery as long as there is daylight. The backup circuit should exclude heavy loads such as electric ovens and car chargers.

## Maintenance and warranty
The battery needs no routine maintenance apart from keeping the air vents free of dust and leaves. Firmware updates are installed automatically over Wi-Fi at night. The warranty lasts ten years and guarantees that the battery will retain at least 70 percent of its original capacity. A blinking red status light indicates a fault, and the owner should then contact support and avoid opening the cover.
