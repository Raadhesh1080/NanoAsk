# Brightwater Family Clinic: Patient Information

## Appointments
Routine appointments can be booked online, by phone or at the front desk, and usually last 15 minutes. Patients who need to discuss several problems should book a double appointment of 30 minutes. Please arrive ten minutes early to update your details. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are recorded as missed, and after three missed appointments in a year a patient may be asked to register elsewhere.

## Same-day care
A number of same-day slots are released every morning at 8 am for urgent but non-emergency problems. Children under five with a fever are always seen on the same day. For chest pain, severe bleeding or difficulty breathing, call the emergency number instead of contacting the clinic.

## Prescriptions
Repeat prescriptions should be requested at least three working days before the medication runs out. Requests can be made through the patient app or by dropping off the repeat slip. Controlled medicines can only be issued for a maximum of 30 days at a time. The clinic pharmacist reviews every long-term medication once a year.

## Tests and results
Blood samples are taken between 8 am and 11 am on weekdays, because samples must reach the hospital laboratory the same day. Some blood tests require fasting for 12 hours beforehand, during which only water is allowed. Most results are available within five working days and appear in the patient app; a doctor will phone the patient directly if a result needs urgent action.

## Vaccinations
Flu vaccinations are offered every autumn to adults over 65, pregnant women and people with long-term conditions. Travel vaccines should be arranged at least eight weeks before departure, since some require more than one dose. The clinic keeps a record of every vaccine given and can print a certificate on request.
