# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

## Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used before the end of March. Sick leave is separate: employees get 10 paid sick days per year, and a doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their 90-day probation period. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the team lead and the head of people operations. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of 600 euros.

## Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the internal travel portal. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 euros per night in major cities and 130 euros elsewhere. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days. Meals during travel are covered by a daily allowance of 45 euros.

## Equipment
Every engineer receives a laptop that is replaced every three years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT support within 48 hours.
