# Riverside Public Library Membership Rules

## Membership
Library cards are free for anyone who lives, works or studies in Riverside County. Applicants must show a photo ID and proof of address issued within the last 60 days. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to sign the application form. Cards must be renewed every two years at any branch.

## Borrowing
Members may borrow up to 15 items at a time. Books and audiobooks are lent for three weeks, while DVDs and video games are lent for one week. Items can be renewed twice online unless another member has placed a hold on them. New releases marked with a yellow sticker cannot be renewed.

## Fines and lost items
The library no longer charges overdue fines on children's materials. Adult materials accrue a fine of 25 cents per day, capped at 5 dollars per item. An item that is 45 days overdue is considered lost, and the member is billed the replacement cost plus a processing fee of 8 dollars. Borrowing privileges are suspended once a member owes more than 20 dollars.

## Study rooms and services
Group study rooms seat up to eight people and can be reserved for two hours per day. Reservations open seven days in advance. Black-and-white printing costs 10 cents per page and color printing costs 50 cents per page. The Main Street branch offers free one-on-one computer tutoring every Saturday morning.

## Opening hours
All branches are open from 9 am to 8 pm on weekdays and from 10 am to 5 pm on Saturdays. The Main Street branch is the only branch open on Sundays, from noon to 4 pm. Branches close on public holidays, and the book drop remains available around the clock.
